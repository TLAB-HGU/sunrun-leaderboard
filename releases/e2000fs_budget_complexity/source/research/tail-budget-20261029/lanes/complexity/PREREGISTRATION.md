## Mechanism
EDS (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02277) motivates information/complexity versus density. This is an EDS-inspired bounded weighting adaptation, not its simplex/interpolation selection algorithm. No row deletion or output blending. Fixed parent inputs (237 ordered columns/block), three 24-vector XGBoost models, 900 rounds/depth3/lr .03/seed42, original subsampling/regularization and scalar unweighted block mean initialization. CPU3/BLAS1. ACE lag0. Every stage uses origin+144h<=fit cutoff for all 72 targets' +72h maturity.

The sole changed mechanism is target-specific training weights. At each fit, split chronologically ordered train origins into four equal-count quarters. First quarter is neutral warmup. For quarters 2–4, train three auxiliary point4 vector predictors on strictly preceding origins satisfying origin+144h<=quarter start; same parent settings except fixed 100 auxiliary rounds (2 smoke). Predict that quarter without future training rows. No validation/official label participates in estimator fitting. All auxiliary cost and per-round losses are recorded separately; final predictor always 900 rounds.

Density context uses only three already-selected causal constant inputs: v_lag0, ace_bt_nt_last, ace_density_cm3_last. Within each forward fold, fit missing-value medians and marginal octile edges on its earlier training rows only. Joint 3D histogram counts +1 smoothing define d=clip(sqrt(median(nonempty historical cell counts)/(query cell count+1)), .5, 2). No 237D geometry. Error e=clip(abs(OOF forecast-y)/100,0,3), including low-speed rising/falling contexts, not y rarity alone. Raw multiplier m=clip(1+.25*(e*d-1),.5,2). Warmup m=1.

Groups are UTC origin calendar year × exact horizon × true speed band (<550 / >=550). For each group, keep warmup rows at multiplier1; if fewer than32 nonwarmup rows keep entire group neutral. Otherwise divide nonwarmup raw multipliers by their mean. Thus group total effective point4 weight is conserved to float precision, warmup stays exactly1, all relative weights remain positive in [.25,4]. No-op multiplier1 returns exact parent float32 weights. Report per-group mass, relative range and ESS; tiny groups use neutral weight. Global horizon normalization remains exactly point4 w0=(4 if y>=550 else1)/mean_train_horizon.
