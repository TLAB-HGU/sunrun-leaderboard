For each stage and block, fit an auxiliary point4 reference on origins+144h<=cutoff-90days; calibrate on origins>=cutoff-90days with origin+144h<=cutoff. These rows/labels are strictly internal to that stage training. Reference budget B=max(1,mean((point4_prediction-y)^2 | y>=550)) on this chronological calibration; no official 6000 threshold. If no calibration high labels, raise infrastructure/data blocker (smoke synthetic tests include highs).
Candidate uses all eligible stage training rows. Let m=1[y>=550], p=mean(m) over all training cells in block, p_floor=max(p,1/Ncells), R=mean(error²), H=mean(error²|m). Lagrangian raw R+lambda*(H-B), nonnegative scalar lambda per block. Rescale each round by (1+lambda) to keep average curvature one: per-cell a=(1+lambda*m/p_floor)/(1+lambda). Objective 0.5*a*error² gives gradient a*error and positive Hessian a (held dual fixed). At p=0 constraint is inactive, lambda=0 and a=1. This is a dynamic constrained-risk objective, not fixed weight tuning or leaf refit.
Initialization lambda0=3*p (initial aggregate high/low ratio4). XGBoost calls objective with pre-round margins and lambda_t; builds structure AND leaves with those derivatives; after_iteration computes post-round TRAIN predictions only and updates lambda_(t+1)=clip(lambda_t+0.02*(H_t/B-1),0,10). All 900 rounds retained, no early stopping. Per-round lambda_used/next, R,H,B, empirical feasibility, train weighted loss, external diagnostic loss and calibration diagnostic loss are saved. Empirical feasibility is distinct from heldout/official feasibility; validation metrics do not update duals.
Control uses exact parent mean-normalized per-horizon point4 objective on identical corrected training rows. No-op identity tests compare control objective/model to immutable parent. Each D1/D2/D3/C/F1/F2 retrains both controls and candidates. Calibration reference is never mixed into final forecasts.
